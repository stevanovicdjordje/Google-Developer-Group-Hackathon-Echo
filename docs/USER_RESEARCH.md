# Eho — User research

> **No primary research recorded yet.** Nothing in this repository is based on user interviews so far. Do not cite findings, quotes or statistics until they are recorded below.

The judges explicitly ask: *Did the team conduct research involving real users?* and *Did the team take the findings into account and adapt the application accordingly?* This file is where that evidence lives.

## Interview guide — attendees

Keep questions neutral. Don't pitch Eho before or during the questions.

1. Tell me about the last conference or event you attended. How did you decide which sessions to go to?
2. What, if anything, was frustrating while you were at the event?
3. How did you find your way around the venue?
4. Was there a talk you couldn't fully follow? What happened?
5. Did you have questions during talks? What did you do with them?
6. Did you meet anyone new? How did that happen? Who did you wish you had met?
7. What information about you would you be comfortable sharing with other attendees? With organizers?
8. What apps or tools, if any, did you use at the event?

## Interview guide — organizers

1. Walk me through how you set up your last event: agenda, speakers, rooms, materials.
2. In what formats does that information exist today (spreadsheets, PDFs, docs)?
3. What do you most want to know during the event? After it?
4. How do you handle audience questions today?
5. What have you tried for attendee apps or engagement? What worked and what didn't?
6. What would you pay for, and who in your organization decides?
7. What are your concerns about AI, recordings or transcripts at your events?
